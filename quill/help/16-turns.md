# Rows and layout

A **turn** is one round of you speaking and Quill writing. Say **"next row"** and the next turn starts.

![The turn page](images/side-panel-turn.png)

## Choose how a turn is written

**1. Click the turn icon** ![turn](images/icon-turn.png) in the toolbar.

*You'll see:* the **Turn Options** panel slide in.

**2. Pick one.**

| Option | Means |
|---|---|
| **Next Row** (the usual) | Every turn is written to a new row. |
| **Append To** | Every turn adds to the **same row**. A cell with a value keeps it and gets the new one after a comma (`Apple, Banana`). |

Click **Next** in the panel when you want the next turn to start a fresh row (Append To only).

**3. Pick the layout** (see [Where your data starts](help:data-starts)): **Columns Across** or **Rows Down**.

## By voice

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| **"Append on"** or **"Append mode"** | Append To |
| **"Append off"** or **"Row mode"** | Next Row |
| **"Rows down"** | Rows Down |
| **"Columns across"** | Columns Across |

A rising sound means on; a lower one means off. The panel follows what you say.

## Good to know

Changing the mode, the file or the sheet starts a fresh row. A cell with a formula is left alone.

Drag the panel's edge to make it wider or narrower; Quill remembers.

The Log and **Last Saved** say *Row 4 (added to)* for these turns.
