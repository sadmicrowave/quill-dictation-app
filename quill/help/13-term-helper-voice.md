# Term Helper by voice

Hands busy? Answer the words by speaking.

## Do this

**1. Click Start Listening in the Term Helper** (or say **"sort terms"**).

*You'll see:* a green glow and moving lines in its Start button.

**2. Say an answer.** Quill answers the top word and moves to the next.

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| **"Yes"** or **"Add"** | Adds it to the suggested column |
| **"Color"** (a column name) | Adds it to that column |
| **"Same as XL"** | Another name for XL |
| **"Ignore"** | Forgets it |
| **"Later"** | Moves it to the end |
| **"Add all"** or **"Ignore all"** | Everything at once |
| **"Undo"** | Takes back the last answer |
| **"Done"** | Leaves voice sorting |

**3. Chain answers in one breath:** *"yes... same as XL... color... done"*.

*You'll see:* each answer shown in the Term Helper, never in the row you are building.

## Good to know

If you say something that isn't a column but sounds like one (*"sighs"* for **Size**), Quill picks that column.

While you answer, Quill listens only for the words an answer is made of, so a TV or a conversation nearby can't answer for you. (Turn off **Focus On Answers While Sorting** in **Settings > Speech Model** to say anything else.)

Quiet for the set time (10 seconds), and voice sorting ends by itself. The words stay in the list.

Click Start in the main window while sorting, and what you say goes into the row again.
