# Numbers and dates

Say them naturally. Quill writes the real number or date.

## Amounts

| You say | Quill writes |
|---|---|
| *"appraisal thirty five"* | 35 in **Appraisal** |
| *"price twelve dollars fifty"* | 12.50 in **Price** |
| *"thirty five dollars"* (no column) | 35 in the sheet's money column. The Log says which. |

## Dates

| You say | Quill writes |
|---|---|
| *"yesterday"*, *"tomorrow"* | That date |
| *"three weeks ago"*, *"a month ago"* | That date |
| *"last friday"*, *"next monday"* | That date |
| *"march third"*, *"october thirty"* | That day, this year (or last year if it is over a month ahead) |
| *"the third of march twenty twenty five"* | 3 March 2025 |

## Which date column?

Quill reads your spreadsheet, not a word list. A word that matches part of a column name picks it (*"departed"* picks **Departure Date**). Otherwise the first empty date column, left to right, is used, and the second date you say goes to the second date column. The Log says which.

To be sure, say the column: *"departure date yesterday"*.

## If something looks off

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| A date in the wrong column | Click the column name in the grid and choose another under **Change this to**. See [Fix a mistake](help:fix-mistake). |
| A word like *"spotted"* always means one column | [Teach Quill your words](help:teach-words). |
