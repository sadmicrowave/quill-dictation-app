# Microphone and speech

Quill listens only while you are listening, and turns speech into text on your computer.

## Check that it hears you

**1. Click Start** ![start](images/icon-play.png) and allow the microphone if asked.

**2. Say a few words.**

*You'll see:* the lines in the Start button's screen move, and your words appear in the box below.

## Improve what it hears

**1. Open Settings** ![settings](images/icon-settings.png) and choose **Speech Model**.

**2. Pick a model and click Install.**

| Model | Size | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Quick** | 40 MB (comes with Quill) | Fast, shows words as you say them |
| **Broad** | 130 MB | Names, unusual words and numbers |
| **Careful** | 145 MB | Best accuracy: each pause, Quill re-reads the phrase |

*You'll see:* a progress bar, then the model selected. It works without an internet connection after that.

You can switch models while dictating from **Turn Options** ![turn](images/icon-turn.png) at the bottom of the panel.

## Good to know

Quill stops listening after **10 quiet seconds** so the microphone is never left open. The button shows the seconds left in a ring. Change the time in **Settings > General**.

**Reduce Background Noise** (on) keeps fans and traffic out of your sheet. Turn it off if Quill misses quiet speech.

## If something looks off

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| Nothing happens when you talk | Allow the microphone in your computer's privacy settings, then click Start. |
| Many words are misheard | Install **Careful** or **Broad**, speak close to the microphone, and pause between phrases. |
| Quill hears its own spoken prompts | Turn off **Speak Sort Term Prompts** in **Settings > General**, or wear headphones. |
