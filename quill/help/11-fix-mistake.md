# Fix a mistake

What to do depends on whether the row is saved yet.

## Before you say "next row"

| You want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Remove one wrong value** | Hover its line in the grid and click the round **x** ![x](images/icon-x.png). It won't be written. |
| **Move a value to another column** | Click the column's name in the grid, then choose another under **Change this to**. |
| **Throw the whole row away** | Say **"cancel"** or **"delete row"**. |
| **Take back a Term Helper answer** | Click **Undo** ![undo](images/icon-undo.png), or say **"undo"**. |
| **Change a value** | Remove its line, then say it again with the column's name: *"color navy"*. |

*You'll see:* the line change or disappear in the grid at once. Nothing is written until **"next row"**.

## After "next row"

A saved row is an ordinary row in your spreadsheet. Fix it there, as you would any cell.

## Good to know

Saying the same column twice keeps the **last** value.

Quill never overwrites a cell that already has something in it.

**Undo** takes back the last 100 Term Helper answers. It never overwrites something you typed since.

## If something looks off

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Undo** does nothing | There is nothing left to undo, or you changed sheets. |
| Nothing is typed into the row | You may still be sorting words by voice: say **"done"**. |
