# Teach it your words

Only you know that *"spotted"* means **Observation Date**. Quill ships with no word list; it learns yours.

## Teach it a word

**1. Say the word, then "means", then the column:** *"spotted means observation date"*.

*You'll see:* it learned at once.

Or correct it as you go:

**1. Click the column's name in the grid** and choose another column under **Change this to**.

**2. Answer Quill's question,** *"Remember that 'spotted' means Observation Date?"*, with **Yes** or **No**.

*You'll see:* after enough corrections (2 by default) Quill uses the word by itself, and the Log says so.

## Good to know

Quill never learns numbers, small everyday words, or words already in your sheet. A column you name always wins.

**Undo** after a change puts the row back and takes back what it taught.

## See and undo what it learned

Open **Settings** ![settings](images/icon-settings.png) > **Learned Words**. Each word shows its column, file, status and how often it was used. Select words and click **Forget Selected** to remove them.

You can also change how many corrections it takes (1 to 5) and turn the question off there. The **Learned Words** link under the active file opens this page for that file.
