# Quick start

Your first saved row, in five steps.

## Do this

**1. Open your spreadsheet** in Excel, Numbers or LibreOffice.

*You'll see:* your sheet's columns listed, muted, in the Quill window within a few seconds.

**2. Click Start** ![start](images/icon-play.png)

The first time, allow the **microphone** when your computer asks. On a Mac, also allow Quill to control your spreadsheet program.

*You'll see:* the button turn darker purple, with moving lines in its screen.

**3. Say what describes the item.** For example: *"black t-shirt, large, good, appraisal thirty five"*.

*You'll see:* your words appear in the box under Start, and each column fill with its value.

**4. Say "next row".**

*You'll see:* the row appear in your spreadsheet, and the grid clear for the next one.

**5. Say "stop listening"** when you are done.

If you forget, Quill stops by itself after 10 quiet seconds.

## If something looks off

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| **File not connected** on the Start button | Open a spreadsheet, or [use a file directly](help:use-a-file-directly). |
| Nothing happens when you talk | Check the microphone is allowed in your computer's privacy settings. |
| A word goes to the wrong column | Say the column name first: *"color black"*. |
