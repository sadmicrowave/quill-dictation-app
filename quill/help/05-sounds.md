# 🔔 Sounds

Short sounds tell you what happened, so your eyes can stay on your hands.

## 🎧 What each sound means

| Sound | Means |
|---|---|
| **Bright rising chirp** | Saved cleanly (or added, or searched) |
| **Three rising notes** | Some words need sorting |
| **Soft falling blip** | Ignored, undone or deleted |
| **Low buzz** | Problem, or not understood |

## 🎛️ Change them

**Settings > Sounds**

- Pick one of **five sound sets**
- Or a **spoken word**: *Okay*, *Done*, *Got it*
- Or turn confirmations **off**
- **Picking a sound plays it straight away**, so there is no Play button. Spoken words are prepared the first time and then play instantly, like the sounds.

## 🤐 Spoken guidance (off by default)

Quill is silent apart from the sounds above. If you want a voice to guide you through the **Term Helper**, turn on **Speak Sort Term Prompts** in **Settings > General**. The system voice then tells you what to do for each term (*"Which column is that? Say Color, Size, or ignore, or later."*) as it becomes the active row, and asks the add-it question when a row ends. Answer **"yes"** or **"no"** as usual.

Quill uses spoken words nowhere else. (The *Spoken word* choice under **Confirm With** is a different, optional way to confirm what you said.)

> 💡 If you use speakers and Quill hears itself, leave this off or wear headphones.

## 🧩 Sounds for the Term Helper

**Settings > Sounds > Sounds for Term Helper Actions** (on by default) controls the confirmation sound when a term in the Term Helper is added, matched or ignored, including **Add All** and **Ignore All**. Turn it off to keep the Term Helper quiet while the rest of Quill still confirms rows.
