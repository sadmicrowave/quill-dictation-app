# 🔔 Sounds

## Sounds: knowing what Quill did without looking

Quill plays a short sound after what you say, so you can keep your eyes on your hands:

| Sound | Means |
|-------|-------|
| A bright rising chirp | Done: the row was saved cleanly (or something was added or searched) |
| Three rising notes | Some words need sorting. The Term Helper window comes to the front |
| A soft falling blip | Ignored, undone, or row deleted |
| A low buzz | A problem, or Quill didn't understand |

**Settings** also lets you change how Quill looks: light, dark or automatic; one of five **accent colours** (Violet, the default, then Terracotta, Ocean, Forest, Rose); and **Spacing**, *Compact* or *Comfortable* (more breathing room around everything). Changes apply when you press OK.

**Text size** (default 12) **Window opacity** (100% is solid; lower it to see through the whole window, text included) and **Window blur** (frosts what you see through it, so it only shows once opacity is below 100%) are there too. Clicking Quill's menu bar icon only opens its menu; it never starts listening by itself. In the main window, click **Log ▴** to hide the log when you don't need it (click again to bring it back); the line under the row, *Last saved*, always stays on a single line and shows how many fields were saved, with the full list when you hover over it.

Open **Settings** to change the sounds: pick one of five sound sets, or switch to a **spoken word** ("Okay", "Done" or "Got it"), or turn it off. **Play sample** lets you hear your choice. The same Settings page sets how many answers **Undo** remembers (default 100).
