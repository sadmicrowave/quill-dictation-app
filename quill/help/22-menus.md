# Menus and shortcuts

Everything Quill does is also in its menus: the menu bar at the top of the screen on a Mac, and the window's own menu bar on Windows. On Windows use **Ctrl** where a Mac uses **⌘**, and **Alt** for **⌥**.

## Good to know

Items that don't apply are greyed out (Next Row while not listening, Undo with nothing to take back). The wording and check marks follow what Quill is doing. On a Mac the same menus show while Settings, the Term Helper or Help is in front.

The menu bar icon (a rounded square: **outline** idle, **solid** listening) lets you start, stop, show the window or quit.
