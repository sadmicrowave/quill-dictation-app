# Search by voice

Filter your sheet by saying what you want to see.

## Do this

**1. Say "search for" and what to find:** *"search for black blouses under 50"*.

*You'll see:* the sheet filter to matching rows.

**2. Say "clear search"** to see every row again.

*You'll see:* all rows back, and your cursor where it was before the search.

## What you can say

| Say | Quill filters |
|---|---|
| *"black blouses"* | Color is Black and Type is Blouses |
| *"black or navy skirts"* | Black **or** navy, skirts |
| *"under 50"*, *"below 50"*, *"less than 50"* | Less than 50 |
| *"at most 50"*, *"up to 50"* | 50 or less |
| *"over 20"*, *"more than 20"* | More than 20 |
| *"at least 20"* | 20 or more |
| *"exactly 20"* | Exactly 20 |

Different columns must all match. Several values in one column mean any of them.

## Good to know

A number applies to the column you name just before it (*"sale price under 20"*). With no column, it uses the first money column and says so in the Log.

Matching is exact (not "contains"), and dates aren't compared. Words not in your lists are reported in the Log, not guessed.

## If something looks off

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Search is not available" | A `.csv` file can't be filtered. Use a spreadsheet file. |
| Search says it needs the headings | Search needs the column names across the top (**Columns Across**). |
