## Findings

1. **Failed: the main window has two top bars once the Term Helper has appeared.** The ghost buttons (a folder with a red dot, doubled
   chat bubbles, a second Help and undo) sit over the "Quill" title. It was clean at launch and appeared after the Term Helper
   window opened by itself during S1; it stays (checked for over a minute and after clicking the window). The accessibility tree
   shows it is real: **two** sets of the buttons "Turn Settings", "Settings", "Mini View" and "Help" in one window. The **Demo** menu
   also vanished from the menu bar at that point. Not yet reproduced as a headless journey: a candidate invariant is "the main
   window has exactly one top bar" after the Term Helper opens. Evidence: a saved picture of the bar is in the session's
   screenshots; steps: Demo > A Row Being Dictated, let it finish, let the Term Helper open.
2. **Demo "A Row Being Dictated" opens the real microphone.** Room sound became words and the Term Helper filled with junk terms
   (2 to 6). Run it in a quiet room, or say so in the journey. Not a Quill bug.
3. **Numbers jumped to the Config sheet** when a new list word was written, and stayed there. May be intended (it shows where the
   word went); worth a decision.
4. **Numbers' warning window** ("This spreadsheet may look different ... unsupported formulas were replaced") and a **blue triangle in every
   cell of the drop-down columns**: not environmental, as first written. The demo workbook stored its drop-down rules as
   `=Config!$A$2:$A$17`; Excel stores them without the "=" and Numbers reads the "=" as a formula it cannot keep. Found by the user
   from a screenshot; fixed (see the follow-up) and verified in Numbers: no warnings, no triangles.
5. **Every demo run leaves a file**: `Demo Inventory 27.numbers` was written to the iCloud Numbers folder (there are 27 now, from
   earlier runs), because Numbers saves the imported workbook next to its other documents.
